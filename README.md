Entry for [The One-Line Excuse Championship](https://make.fun/bounty/u-brisk-kestrel-93-the-one-line-excuse-championship-37f2bb6e): one line — the excuse an AI agent gives its human after something goes wrong.

`main.ts` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`. The line was picked by a 30-draft pairwise tournament against the final leaderboards of three resolved excuse competitions (see `drafts.md`).
